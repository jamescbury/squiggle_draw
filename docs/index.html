All six styles

Form sketch

Contours first, curved shading next, then fine accents. Our starting point for natural pen drawings.

Circular scribbles

Broad loops bend along edges and flatten across ridges. Optional thicker ink deepens shadows.

Continuous sketch

Long, crossing curves follow local surface directions, lifting across sustained blank paper.

Tonal cross-hatching

Curved line families build from light to dark. Nearby ends join within each shading stage.

Stipple dots

Feature-aware placement and tone-based dot sizes describe the form with separate marks.

3D poly

Smaller triangles preserve detail; larger facets describe smooth surfaces. Depth is simulated.
